# Turn Free Government Data Into Profitable Software

A concise founder guide and interactive directory for building profitable software products using free government data rails (API Setu, Open Government Data, and sovereign benchmarks).

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://rohitsbag.github.io/apisetu-founder-deck/](https://rohitsbag.github.io/apisetu-founder-deck/)

---

## ⚡ What's Inside

- **6 Unfair Moats:** Why building on public data creates defensible monopolies (zero competitor noise, mandatory business demand, 100% genuine digital seals, 85%+ profit margins, zero churn, and ₹0 data cost).
- **3 Simple Data Access Methods:**
  1. *With User Permission (Phone OTP)* — Pull official verified documents (PAN, degrees, marksheets, RC, driving license).
  2. *Instant Public Search (No OTP)* — Instant lookups (business GST status, traffic fines, court cases, company filings).
  3. *Full Database Downloads* — Offline datasets (155k PIN codes, daily mandi crop prices, 2.6M registered companies).
- **Curated Data Directory:**
  - **70+ Live Indian APIs** across Finance, Transport, Education, Identity, Health, Welfare, Land, and Agriculture.
  - **10 National Bulk Databases** (PIN code directory, MCA company master, e-Courts, ISRO Bhuvan satellite maps, Agmarknet).
  - **8 US Proven Benchmarks** (SEC EDGAR, openFDA, NOAA Weather, FRED) for building global products.
- **Profit Calculator:** Interactive slider testing monthly volume, customer revenue (@ ₹49/check), server costs, and take-home profits at ~98% net margin.
- **5 Ready-to-Build Products:**
  1. **VyaparRadar** — Check GST tax history to spot fake vendors before paying advance money (₹499/mo).
  2. **VeriDegree** — Instant background check confirming real college degrees directly from universities (₹99/check).
  3. **VahanDossier** — Check used cars for unpaid loans, true owner count, and traffic fines (₹49/report).
  4. **SchemeHak** — Answer 4 questions to find government scholarships and cash benefits (Advertising).
  5. **TrustPass** — Digital QR pass for apartment tenants proving identity without sharing private Aadhaar cards (₹2,000/mo).
