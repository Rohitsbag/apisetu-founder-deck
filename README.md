# API Setu — Founder Strategic Deck & Visual Opportunity Engine

An interactive, visual executive briefing and product blueprint dashboard exploring the **API Setu** (`apisetu.gov.in`) ecosystem by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights
- **Sovereign Scale:** 2,940 Registered Publishers • 9,163 Live APIs across 18 Domain Categories • 10,409 Active Consumers.
- **The "Boring" Moat:** Why building on sovereign public data rails provides unfair defensibility (zero competitor noise, mandated regulatory compliance, cryptographic Section 9A legal validity, and 95%+ software margins).
- **8 Core Sectors:** Finance & Tax, Transport & Fleet, Education & Academics, Identity & Civic, Health & Wellness, Welfare Schemes, Land & Courts, and Agriculture & Utilities.
- **5 Ready Product Blueprints with UI Mockups:**
  1. **VyaparRadar** — B2B Vendor Due-Diligence & Tax Compliance Shield (₹499/mo SaaS)
  2. **VeriDegree** — 10-Second Candidate Marksheet/Degree BGV for Startups (₹99/audit)
  3. **SchemeHak** — AI Welfare & Grant Matcher for 2,000+ Schemes
  4. **VahanDossier** — Used Vehicle Lemon & Loan Checker (₹49/report)
  5. **TrustPass** — Privacy-Preserving Tenant & Domestic Staff QR Pass
- **Interactive Financial Simulator:** Live margin and cash flow calculator.
